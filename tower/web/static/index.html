              Known WiFi networks
              
Networks the Pi uses to reach the internet for updates, first choice at the top: the tower's WiFi, or a phone hotspot. Phones always connect through the Pi's own access point. With a network cable plugged in, the cable is used instead. With a USB WiFi dongle, the dongle stays connected. With neither, the Pi joins one of these briefly when it starts and when you press Check for updates, then comes back to its access point. Leave the list empty to keep the Pi offline.
